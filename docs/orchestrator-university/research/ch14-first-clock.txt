Chapter 14 first lesson — source and design record
Research performed 2026-09-17. Scope: one 12-minute lesson, simple-harmonic-motion and pendulum-small-angle only.

Authoritative physics consulted before implementation
1. OpenStax, University Physics Volume 1, 15.1 Simple Harmonic Motion
https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/15-1-simple-harmonic-motion
Retrieved readable page via WebFetch. Supports a=−ω²x, ideal-spring T=2π√(m/k), amplitude-independent T, vmax=Aω at equilibrium and maximum acceleration at turning points. Assumptions are Hooke's law and no damping. These are the spring prediction, linked readings and transfer sketch claims.
2. OpenStax, University Physics Volume 1, 15.4 Pendulums
https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/15-4-pendulums
Retrieved readable page. Supports sine restoring force, small-angle substitution and T0=2π√(L/g). The text's roughly 15-degree illustration concerns error in replacing sin θ, NOT a universal period-error threshold. Lesson therefore reports exact computed period excess, and does not turn that angle into a magic cutoff.

Visualization precedents consulted before implementation
3. myPhysicsLab, Pendulum
https://www.myphysicslab.com/pendulum/pendulum-en.html
Retrieved readable documentation; did not operate simulation. Documents draggable initial conditions, gravity/mass/friction controls and simulation/phase/time graph tabs. Design borrowed: one underlying nonlinear trajectory linked to a physical bob and a time trace. Our lesson constrains knobs to release angle and time to keep the failure test focused.
4. PhET, Masses and Springs
https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/masses-and-springs
Fetch exposed only title/topics, not useful controls or learning objectives. NOT evidence that I inspected or ran the simulation. No detailed design claim relies on it.

Design decisions and scope
- First commitment precedes the widget, which sits outside Predict so no nested island relies on hidden-slot hydration.
- Learner-paced time scrub rather than autoplay. Changing release distance preserves time: peaks change height while their times stay fixed. Moving mass breaks that invariance.
- Physical spring + circling projection + displacement history + numerical x/v/a are linked to one analytic state. New left-release velocity sketch changes representation, sign, stiffness and numbers.
- Pendulum solid trajectory integrates the sine force. It is NOT a cosine with an exact period substituted. Dashed approximation has the same release angle, ω=2; g/L=4 is explicitly disclosed.
- PotentialTrack not used: the first lesson does not need energy, and existing source has unrelated limitations (zero start velocity for arbitrary startX, artificial reflection at domain edges, invisible zero-width energy bars, and no numeric axis ticks). Not modified; parent notified.
- Dark-only project tokens; iris and magenta at 80% sRGB mixed with black. Palette validator passes all checks (#727dc4,#cc3e7e against #14111f). Initial unmodified cyan/orchid pair failed the skill lightness band; it was not shipped. Solid/dashed and filled/hollow secondary encodings, labels, hover cursor, keyboard sliders, and sampled-value table.
- No new graded widget: established Predict/SketchCurve supply progress, new visualization is ungraded and automatically registered by its own wrapper.
