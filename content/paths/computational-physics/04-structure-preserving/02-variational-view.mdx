## Planned outline

1. The continuous action, and least action as the source of the equations of motion.
2. A discrete Lagrangian, and the discrete Euler-Lagrange equations.
3. Recovering velocity Verlet from a midpoint discrete Lagrangian - it was variational all along.
4. The discrete Noether theorem: conserved momenta to machine precision, not to O(h^p).
5. Where this beats symplectic-by-construction: constraints, contact, non-smooth mechanics.
