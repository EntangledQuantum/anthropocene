## Planned outline

1. Differentiation as multiplication by ik in Fourier space.
2. Spectral accuracy: error falling faster than any power of h for smooth periodic functions.
3. Why one discontinuity destroys it - the Gibbs phenomenon.
4. Chebyshev bases for non-periodic domains.
5. Split-step and pseudo-spectral methods for nonlinear PDEs.
6. The cost: global coupling, FFT-shaped grids, hard boundary conditions.
