<Step>

## Name it

The creaking ropes are **damping**: a force against the velocity that leaks energy from
every cycle. A drive in step with the natural rhythm puts energy in every cycle. That is
**resonance**:

$$\ddot x + 2\beta\,\dot x + \omega_0^2\,x = f_0\cos\omega t$$

The swing stops growing once the leak through $2\beta\dot x$ matches what the drive supplies
each cycle. So the peak is finite, about $Q = \omega_0/2\beta$ times the hand's own
movement. Halve the damping and the peak doubles.

<Recall id="up-ch14-recall-resonance" concept="resonance">
  <div slot="front">A spring is shaken at exactly its natural rate. Why doesn't the swing grow forever?</div>

  Damping drains more energy per cycle the bigger the swing gets. The swing grows until
  that drain equals what the drive puts in each cycle, then holds. The peak is set by the
  damping: roughly $Q$ times what the same shaking gives at a very slow rate.
</Recall>

## Damping with the wrong sign

A model deck in a wind tunnel. Its wind pushes in proportion to how fast the deck is
already twisting, so the wind has no rhythm of its own.

<FlutterDeck
  id="up-ch14-flutter-onset"
  prompt="Find the slowest steady wind at which a small twist grows instead of dying."
  explanation="About 18 m/s for this deck. Below that, the wind's push with the motion is weaker than the deck's own damping, and the twist dies. Above it, the damping is effectively negative and each twist is bigger than the last. The deck twists at its own rate whatever the wind speed. This is flutter, not resonance."
/>

<Recall id="up-ch14-recall-flutter" concept="damped-oscillation">
  <div slot="front">A steady wind makes a bridge deck twist harder and harder. In terms of damping, what is the wind doing?</div>

  Supplying damping of the wrong sign. Its push follows the deck's twisting velocity, so it
  adds energy every cycle instead of removing it. Once that beats the structure's own
  damping, any small twist grows, at the deck's own frequency.
</Recall>

## What to carry forward

- **A push adds energy only along the motion.** Damping is a push against it, all the time.
- **Resonance is a drive in step with the natural rhythm.** It is limited by damping, and
  flutter is what happens when the damping turns negative.
