## The charge crowds to the glass

Stop with the glass part way in. The plates' charge rearranges itself, but it cannot leave the plates.

  targetShare={0.5}
  explanation="About a sixth of the way in. The field is the same all across the gap, V/d, and beside the glass the plate needs five times the charge to make it, because the glass’s bound charge cancels four fifths. So a sixth covered by glass holds as much as five sixths of bare air. The + plate still holds 88.5 nC; it has only moved along the plate."

<Step>

## Name it

The glass raised the charge the plates hold per volt by its **dielectric constant**, κ = 5:

$$C = \kappa\,\dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d}$$

From 88.5 pF to 443 pF, without moving a plate. With the battery disconnected, V fell by κ; had it stayed connected, it would have pushed in κ times the charge instead.

<Recall id="up-ch24-recall-dielectric" concept="dielectric">
  <div slot="front">A capacitor is left connected to its battery while you slide glass into the gap. Its charge goes up five times. Where did the extra charge come from?</div>

  From the battery. The glass makes no charge: it stays neutral, with its molecules only
  turned. It lowered the voltage the plates' charge produces, so the battery pushed on more
  until the plates were back at its voltage.
</Recall>

</Step>

## Fill it before it sparks

Every insulator has a field it cannot take: past it, the gap tears open in a spark and dumps
everything. You have a charger, the same 1 mm gap, and the glass.

<Step>

## The energy is in the gap

The wash filled the gap, not the plates: every cubic metre of field holds

$$u = \tfrac12\,\kappa\,\varepsilon_0 E^2$$

Summed over the gap's volume, $Ad$, that is exactly $\tfrac12 CV^2$. The breakdown field caps $E$, and so caps the energy.

  <div slot="front">With the charge fixed, you double the gap of a capacitor. The charges on the plates are unchanged. Why does the stored energy double?</div>

  Because the energy is in the field, not on the plates. The field in the gap is unchanged, so

## What to carry forward

- **A dielectric makes no charge.** Its molecules turn, their bound charge cancels part of the field, and C rises by κ.
- **The energy lives in the field**, ½κε₀E² per cubic metre. Breakdown caps E, and so caps the energy.
- **What stays put depends on the wire**: disconnected, Q; connected, V.

Next: give the charge a wire to move along, and it becomes a current.
