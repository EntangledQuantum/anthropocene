## Find what is missing

two alone do not balance the books.

<Step>

## Name it

Every particle carries three counts: charge $Q$, baryon number $B$ and lepton number $L$, and
its antiparticle carries each with the opposite sign. A reaction happens only if every total is
the same after as before, and a decay only if the products weigh no more than the parent.

| | $Q$ | $B$ | $L$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| p, n | +1, 0 | +1 | 0 |
| e⁻, νₑ | −1, 0 | 0 | +1 |
| γ, π⁰ | 0 | 0 | 0 |

<Recall id="up-ch44-recall-baryon" concept="baryon-number">
  <div slot="front">A free neutron decays in minutes. Why can a free proton not do the same?</div>

  Both carry baryon number 1, so each must leave a baryon behind. The neutron is heavier than
  the proton and can turn into it. The proton is the lightest baryon: there is nothing lighter
  for it to become.
</Recall>

</Step>

## An antiproton never comes alone

Two proton beams meet head on, each with the same kinetic energy. You want an antiproton,
whose rest energy is 938 MeV, the same as a proton's.

<CollideForAntiproton
  id="up-ch44-least-energy-antiproton"
  prompt="Fire at the least energy per beam that makes an antiproton."
  explanation="Just over 938 MeV per beam: a whole proton's worth each, not half. A lone p̄ would turn charge +2 into +1 and baryon number 2 into 1, so it must leave with a new proton beside it, and the collision pays for two masses. Against a proton at rest the same pair costs 5.6 GeV, because the debris must also carry the beam's momentum. The Bevatron at Berkeley was built to reach 6.2 GeV for exactly this reason, and found the antiproton in 1955."
/>

<Recall id="up-ch44-recall-antiparticle" concept="antiparticle">
  <div slot="front">A collision has made an antiproton. What else, at the least, must it have made?</div>

  Something that puts charge +1 and baryon number +1 back: in practice a proton. Antimatter is
  made in step with matter, which is why a universe made almost entirely of matter is still an
  open puzzle.
</Recall>

## What to carry forward

- **A reaction is a ledger.** Charge, baryon number and lepton number are the same before and
  after, and a decay needs the products to weigh no more than the parent.
- **A count that will not balance points at a missing particle**, or at a reaction that never
  happens.
- **Antimatter is made with matter**, so making a p̄ pays for two masses.
