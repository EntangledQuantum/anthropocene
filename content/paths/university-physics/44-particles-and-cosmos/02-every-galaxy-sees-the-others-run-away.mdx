<Step>

## Name it

Every distance grows by the same fraction each second, so from every galaxy speed is
proportional to distance. That is **Hubble's law**, and its slope is the Hubble constant; a
megaparsec is 3.3 million light-years.

$$v = H_0\, d,\qquad H_0 \approx 70\ \text{km/s per Mpc}$$

<Recall id="up-ch44-recall-hubble" concept="hubble-law">
  <div slot="front">Astronomers in a galaxy 500 Mpc from us measure the Milky Way's motion. What do they see?</div>

  The Milky Way receding from them at about 35,000 km/s, exactly as fast as we see them recede.
  Each sees the other run away. Neither is the centre.
</Recall>

</Step>

<Step>

## Light stretches too

The sheet stretches whatever crosses it, light included. Light left glowing 3000 K gas
everywhere at once, when the young universe cooled enough to become transparent.

<StretchTheLight
  id="up-ch44-stretch-to-microwaves"
  prompt="Stretch the universe until this light is today's 2.7 K sky."
  explanation="About 1100 times. The wavelength grew 1100-fold, from about 1 µm to about 1 mm, and each photon kept 1/1100 of its energy. That is the cosmic microwave background: leftover light from a hot universe, arriving from every direction because the hot gas was everywhere."
/>

<Recall id="up-ch44-recall-redshift" concept="cosmological-redshift">
  <div slot="front">A photon left a galaxy when the universe was a third of its present size. What has happened to its wavelength, and to its energy?</div>

  Its wavelength is three times longer, $1 + z = 3$, and it carries a third of the energy it
  left with.
</Recall>

## What does not stretch

Hubble's law describes things that nothing holds together. Andromeda is 0.78 Mpc from us.

## What to carry forward

- **No centre.** Every distance grows by the same factor, so every galaxy sees $v = H_0 d$, and
  about $1/H_0 \approx 14$ billion years ago every distance was zero, everywhere at once.
- **Light stretches with the sheet:** $1 + z = a_{\text{now}}/a_{\text{then}}$. The microwave sky
  is 3000 K light stretched 1100 times.
- **The ledger and the sheet** are the reusable pictures: what can happen is decided by what is
  conserved, and the largest thing that happened is still stretching.

That closes the path. Every lesson in it made the same move, finding what stays the same and
letting it decide what can happen, and the universe turned out to obey it on every scale.
