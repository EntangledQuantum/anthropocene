<Step>

## Name it

Where two waves overlap, the rope's height is the sum of theirs, and each carries on untouched:

$$y(x,t) = y_1(x,t) + y_2(x,t)$$

At the flat instant none of the energy is in stretch. All of it, every joule, is in motion.

<Recall id="up-ch15-recall-flat" concept="wave-superposition">
  <div slot="front">Two equal and opposite pulses overlap and the rope is exactly flat. Where is their energy?</div>

  All in motion. The flat rope is not stretched, but its beads are moving at their fastest,
  and a moment later the two pulses re-emerge and carry on.
</Recall>

## The rope picks the notes

Only patterns with a still point at each end fit, so the loops come in half-wavelengths,
$\lambda_n = 2L/n$:

$$f_n = \frac{n\,v}{2L} = \frac{n}{2L}\sqrt{\frac{F}{\mu}}$$

## What to carry forward

- **Waves add and pass through.** The rope takes the sum of the heights, and each wave leaves unchanged.
- **A standing wave is two travellers**, one out and one back, adding to loops that stay put.
- **The rope sets its notes**: $f_n = n v / 2L$. The source only picks which one it drives.
